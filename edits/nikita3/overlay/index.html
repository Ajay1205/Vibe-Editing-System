WORD 01 / 03
ACTUALLY
Matlab Asal mein
01 ACTUALLY = Asal mein
☕
EXAMPLE
Actually, I don't like coffee.
Asal mein mujhe coffee pasand nahi hai
📍
EXAMPLE
Actually, I am from Delhi.
Asal mein main Delhi se hoon
💡 Yaad rakhiye
Koi baat clarify karni ho
ya sach kehna ho
→ shuru karo Actually se
WORD 02 / 03
INSTEAD
Matlab Iske bajaye / Uski jagah
02 INSTEAD = Iske bajaye / Uski jagah
🍵
EXAMPLE
Let's have tea instead of coffee.
Coffee ki bajaye chai peete hain
🚶‍♀️
EXAMPLE
I walked instead of taking a taxi.
Taxi lene ki bajaye main paidal chali gayi
💡 Yaad rakhiye
2 options mein se 1 choose karna ho
→ bolo Instead / Instead of
WORD 03 / 03
FINALLY
Matlab Aakhirkaar / Ant mein
03 FINALLY = Aakhirkaar / Ant mein
✅
EXAMPLE
Finally, I finished my work.
Aakhirkaar maine apna kaam poora kar diya
🚌
EXAMPLE
Finally, the bus arrived.
Aakhirkaar bus aa gayi
💡 Yaad rakhiye
Lamba intezaar khatam ho jaaye
→ bolo Finally
